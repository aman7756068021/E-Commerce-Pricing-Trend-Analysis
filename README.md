# **📊 E-Commerce Pricing Analysis: Amazon vs Flipkart**

## **🚀 Project Overview**
This project focuses on monitoring **pricing trends** across major e-commerce platforms to analyze **competitor pricing strategies**, identify **market trends**, and evaluate **promotional activities**. By scraping product pricing data from **Amazon** and **Flipkart**, we gain **actionable insights** into price fluctuations, discount strategies, and consumer demand patterns.

## **🔍 Key Objectives**
✅ **Compare** product pricing across platforms (**Amazon vs Flipkart**).  
✅ **Track** price fluctuations and promotional strategies over time.  
✅ **Analyze** revenue, cost, and profitability by category and region.  
✅ **Visualize** insights through interactive dashboards in **Power BI / Tableau**.  

## **📌 Project Workflow**

### **1️⃣ Data Collection**
🔹 Scrape pricing data from e-commerce platforms like **Amazon** & **Flipkart**.  
🔹 Capture product categories: **Electronics, Clothing, Home & Kitchen, Books, Beauty**.  
🔹 Extract details such as **price, discounts, payment methods, and availability**.  

### **2️⃣ Data Cleaning & Processing**
🔹 **Standardize product names** and remove inconsistencies.  
🔹 **Normalize prices** by considering taxes, shipping fees, and different currencies.  
🔹 **Handle missing values** and filter out duplicate entries.  

### **3️⃣ Competitor Pricing Analysis**
🔹 Compare **Amazon vs Flipkart** pricing across different product categories.  
🔹 Track **seasonal promotions** (**Black Friday, Big Billion Days, Festive Sales**).  
🔹 Identify pricing patterns and promotional tactics.  

### **4️⃣ Data Visualization & Insights**
🔹 Build **interactive dashboards** in **Power BI / Tableau**.  
🔹 Visualize **price fluctuations**, **discount trends**, and **stock levels**.  
🔹 Identify top-selling products and price leadership strategies.  

## **🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills**
- **🖥 Web Scraping** → BeautifulSoup, Scrapy, Selenium  
- **📊 Data Processing** → Pandas, NumPy  
- **📦 Database Management** → SQL, PostgreSQL  
- **📉 Data Visualization** → Power BI, Tableau, Matplotlib, Seaborn  



## **📈 Expected Outcomes**
📌 Better understanding of **Amazon vs Flipkart** pricing strategies.  
📌 Identification of **market trends and consumer behavior**.  
📌 Interactive **dashboard insights** for business decision-making.
